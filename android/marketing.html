Full description

English (US)

Bring beautiful pictures together in Puzzle Bloom, a relaxing picture-puzzle game. Drag and match picture pieces to complete scenes, clear the board, and discover more levels. Enjoy botanical artwork, gentle sound effects, and satisfying animations as each picture comes together. Features: - Picture puzzles with simple touch controls - Hint and Magic tools to help when you get stuck - Combo feedback and end-of-level ratings - Music, sound, and vibration settings - Progress saved locally on your device - No account required The first 20 levels are included with the app. Additional level content downloads as you progress and requires an internet connection when it is not already cached.
