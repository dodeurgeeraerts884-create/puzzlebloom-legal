Description

English (US) · 1175/4000

Make room for a little puzzle time with Puzzle Bloom. Match picture pieces, complete beautiful scenes, and watch new cards drop into place. Explore a collection of nature, cozy spaces, animals, and everyday moments through 100 levels of picture-matching play. LOOK, MATCH, DISCOVER Find matching pieces on the board and bring each picture together. Simple touch controls make it easy to start, while changing layouts keep you looking for the next match. A LITTLE HELP WHEN YOU NEED IT Use Hint to spot a possible match, or Magic to help clear a pair. Each tool uses an available item when successfully activated. MAKE IT YOUR MOMENT Enjoy a soft botanical setting, satisfying card animations, and music and sound controls. Adjust vibration in the settings to suit the way you play. PICK UP WHERE YOU LEFT OFF Your progress and settings are saved on your device. No account is required. The first 20 levels are included with the app. Levels 21–100 download as you progress, so an internet connection is required for content not already on your device. Mobile-data charges may apply. Progress is stored locally and may be lost if the app is deleted or you change devices.
